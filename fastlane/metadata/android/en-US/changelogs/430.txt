PGPony 4.3.0 adds full GnuPG composite-key interoperability: import, decrypt, and export gpg composite keys, including for GPG4WIN. Key management gains a recycle bin with restore, changing a key's passphrase, and a session policy that can hold a passphrase until the phone locks. Also new: animated QR for large keys, optional .zip output for transport, editable key notations, and Russian, the seventh language. iOS parity lands later.
